Why Sofia is different

Most AI writing tools optimise for sounding good. Sofia optimises for being right when someone checks.

It will not invent a number

Every figure is either something you supplied, something derived with the arithmetic shown, or a visible gap marked for you to fill. Sofia never fills a blank with something plausible.

It shows its working

Computations are line by line. Claims cite their source. Every document ends with a register of what it assumed and what would break if the assumption is wrong.

It knows where it stops

Sofia prepares documents. It does not file your return, sign your contract, or act as your lawyer — and it tells you plainly when you need someone who can.
